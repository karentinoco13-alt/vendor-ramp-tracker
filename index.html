Vendor Ramp Tracker

A 90-day proficiency console for outsourced teams. Anyone can answer a few questions to choose the metrics that matter for their vendor. Then they log results and see, in plain language, whether the team is on pace, needs coaching, or has reached proficiency at day 90.

How it scores

From a few questions to a day-90 verdict

  1. Answer the setup questionsVendor, start date, type of work and priorities. The answers filter a library of metrics and recommend the ones that fit.
  2. Pick metrics and set goalsUse the suggested metrics or add your own. Each one gets a goal, a direction (higher or lower is better) and a weight from 1 to 3.
  3. Choose a rampGradual, steady or fast. The ramp sets each checkpoint's target as a share of the goal, for example 70%, 85% and 100% at days 30, 60 and 90.
  4. Log resultsEnter values daily or weekly. Each value is compared with the target for its phase. At or above target is on track, 90–99% is watch, and below 90% is at risk.
  5. Get the verdictThe weighted score for days 61–90 decides proficiency. The team needs 100% or more overall, with no single metric below 90%.

Attainment

Higher is better: actual ÷ target. Lower is better: target ÷ actual. For lower-is-better metrics, early targets are looser (goal ÷ ramp %).

Weighted score

Σ(weight × attainment) ÷ Σ weights. Each metric is capped at 120%, so one strong metric can't hide a weak one.

Coaching alerts

Any metric at risk in the current phase raises an alert with a suggested next step for its focus area, so the review ends with an action.

About this project

Built to make vendor onboarding reviews consistent and reusable across teams. The data exports as tidy CSV files for Tableau, and the data model maps directly onto Salesforce custom objects for a live feed. See the Tableau export tab.
